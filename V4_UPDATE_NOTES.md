# V4 Update Notes

## Product decisions

### Calendar is a separate tab
The Calendar is not embedded in the daily reading flow. It is a separate top-level view accessed from the navigation tabs.

### Favorite applies to the whole day
A favorite represents the day's full poem / essay / artwork combination rather than one media item. This keeps the interaction simple and lets the Calendar become a personal archive of standout days.

### Reset and favorite are independent
Resetting an unfinished day clears the reflection and progress state only. Favorite status remains, because a user may still want to remember the selected material even if they want to restart the day's reflection.

### Darker aesthetic
Mahogany is now the foundation color. Maroon and phthalo green remain the expressive accents, with cream text and muted-gold favorite markers for contrast.
